SERVICES

* Website and digital menu

“Open even when the kitchen is closed”

A fast website designed for the phone, where customers find the menu, the opening hours and everything they need to choose your restaurant.

  • Digital menu that you update with a single message
  • In Italian, German and English for tourists
  • Opening hours, map, WhatsApp, daily specials and events

* Online bookings

“The table books itself”

Customers book at any hour, from your website or from Google, without calling. The phone stays free during service.

  • Booking from your website and your Google listing, day and night
  • The customer picks the day, the time and the number of guests
  • A confirmation to the customer and an alert to you for every booking
  • Integration with the booking software you already use, to be assessed together

* Social media

“Dishes worth looking at”

Instagram and Facebook run by people who do this for a living, with photos of your dishes and your restaurant.

  • Strategy and post calendar, agreed with you
  • Photos and reels on site, with dedicated shoots
  • Stories, replies to comments and messages
  • Local Meta campaigns for aperitifs, events and seasonal menus

* Google and visibility

“They find you before the others”

When someone searches for “restaurant near me”, you need to be there, with photos, opening hours and reviews in order.

  • Optimised Google Business Profile, with photos and a “Book” button
  • Reviews: automatic requests and AI-assisted replies, checked by a person
  • Local SEO for people searching in German and English
  • Visibility on ChatGPT and AI Overviews, monthly report

* AI and automation

“Repetitive work, on autopilot”

Chatbots and automatic workflows that answer customers and handle repetitive tasks, even outside opening hours, so you have more time for service.

  • Website chatbot that answers questions on opening hours, menu and bookings, even when the kitchen is closed
  • Requests for groups and events collected and organised automatically
  • Booking confirmations and reminders sent automatically
  • Together we pick the repetitive tasks worth automating, starting with the most useful

FAQ

Q: How much does it cost?

A: Fixed prices, no surprises. On the call, after seeing your restaurant, we tell you what it costs and how long it takes, before you sign anything.

Q: Do you do photo shoots of the restaurant and the dishes?

A: Yes: we come to you to photograph the restaurant, the dishes and the people who work there, so your website and social media start with real images, not stock photos. The day is agreed together, at a time when the restaurant is quiet.

Q: How do online bookings work?

A: The customer books from your website or your Google listing, choosing the day, the time and the number of guests, without calling. If you already use booking software, we'll talk about it on the call.

Q: Can I update the menu myself?

A: You can, but you don't have to: send us a message or a photo of the new menu and we'll update it.

Q: Do you run the social media?

A: Yes: post calendar, photos and reels of your restaurant, and replies to messages. We agree everything with you, and nothing goes online without a person having reviewed it.

Q: How long does it take to go online?

A: About ten working days from the call. Most of the time goes into collecting photos and opening hours, which is why we only ask you for a ten-minute form.
